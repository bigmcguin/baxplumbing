Services

Plumbing, gas fitting and drainage for homes, businesses and industrial sites in Mount Isa. All work is done by licensed tradespeople.

Hot water systems

No hot water, water that never gets hot enough, or a tank leaking at the base. We fix the system you have or replace it with one that suits your household and your power bill.

  • Electric, gas and solar hot water systems
  • Repairs to thermostats, elements, valves and leaks
  • Advice on size and type for your house

Gas fitting

Gas work in Queensland must be done by a licensed gas fitter. We install and repair gas lines and appliances and check them for leaks.

  • New gas lines and connections
  • Cooktops, ovens, heaters and gas hot water
  • Gas leak detection and repairs
  • Bottle and regulator set-ups

If you can smell gas, turn it off at the meter or bottle, open windows, keep away from flames and switches, then call us.

Blocked drains

Slow sinks, gurgling toilets or water backing up in the yard usually mean a blockage further down the line. We clear it and tell you what caused it.

  • Electric drain machine for roots and tough blockages
  • High-pressure hydro jetter to scour pipes clean
  • Kitchen, bathroom, toilet, sewer and stormwater drains

Drain camera inspections

A waterproof camera goes down the pipe so we can see the problem on a screen instead of guessing. It saves digging up the wrong part of the yard.

  • Find blockages, tree roots, cracks and collapsed pipes
  • Locate pipes before you build or landscape
  • Footage to show you, your landlord or your insurer

Sewer and stormwater drainage

New drainage for extensions and new builds, and repairs or replacement of old and broken lines.

  • Sewer connections and drainage for new builds and extensions
  • Repairs to broken, sunken or root-damaged pipes
  • Stormwater drainage and downpipe connections

Backflow prevention

Backflow devices stop contaminated water being drawn back into the town supply. Many commercial and industrial properties are required to have them.

  • Supply and installation of backflow prevention devices
  • Testing and maintenance
  • Advice on what your property needs

General plumbing and maintenance

The everyday jobs that still need doing properly.

  • Leaking taps, toilets and showers
  • Burst and leaking pipes
  • Bathroom, kitchen and laundry plumbing
  • Renovation plumbing
  • Planned maintenance for commercial and industrial sites
